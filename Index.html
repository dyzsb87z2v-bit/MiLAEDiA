Editorial Statement

A handwoven silk carpet is not decoration. It is the record of a lifetime spent at the loom.

MiLAEDiA gathers antique and master-workshop carpets from Tabriz, Isfahan, Qum and Kashan — pieces of six hundred to twelve hundred knots to the square inch, tied on looms that have not changed in four centuries. Every carpet reaches Berlin with its weaver, its workshop and its date established before it is ever offered.
